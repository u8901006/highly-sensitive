Today's literature shows a notable shift toward translational and clinical applications of Sensory Processing Sensitivity (SPS), with several studies exploring differential susceptibility frameworks, clinical interventions, and neural correlates. There's growing interest in how SPS interacts with other conditions (ADHD, autism, eating disorders) and how it can be leveraged in therapeutic contexts. The methodological quality varies, but the focus on real-world clinical utility is strengthening.
